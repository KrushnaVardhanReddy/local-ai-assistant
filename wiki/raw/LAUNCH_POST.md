**Title:** I built an invisible AI Interview Assistant to replace $150/mo subscriptions

**Body:**

I was tired of seeing job seekers get gouged by "AI Interview Assistants" charging insane $50-$150/month subscriptions. Most of them are slow, laggy, and risk your privacy by sending your voice to their cloud servers. 

So I built a completely private, one-time payment alternative that runs directly on your computer. 

**Here is what it does:**
*   **100% Invisible:** The floating app window bypasses screen-recording software. It is completely invisible to Zoom, Teams, and Google Meet if you are asked to share your screen.
*   **Zero-Delay Answers:** Because it processes your audio locally, the AI starts feeding you answers the exact second the interviewer stops talking. No awkward pauses.
*   **Upload Your Own Resume:** You can upload your resume, job description, or company research. The AI reads it instantly and tailors every answer to your exact background. 
*   **Live Web Search:** If you get asked about a feature that came out yesterday, the AI will silently search the internet during the interview to get you the right answer.
*   **Remote Helper Mode:** Don't want to touch your keyboard during the interview? Generate a secure link, send it to a friend, and they can silently inject code snippets from the chat, trigger web searches, or **clear your screen for you** from another city while your hands stay perfectly still. 

You just bring your own OpenAI API key (which costs pennies per interview) or use a completely free local model like Ollama. No $150 monthly subscriptions.
