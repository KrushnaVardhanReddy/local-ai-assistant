# Launch Strategy

This document outlines the marketing and launch strategy for Project Parakeet.

## Core Value Proposition
The main pitch to users: **"An invisible AI Interview Assistant to replace $150/mo subscriptions."**

The marketing angle focuses heavily on the pain points of existing solutions:
- **Price**: Avoiding expensive recurring subscriptions by offering a BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) or local model approach.
- **Privacy & Security**: Assuring users that their voice isn't sent to a cloud server unless they opt-in to a cloud API.
- **Stealth & Speed**: Emphasizing the zero-delay answers (via local processing) and the 100% invisible desktop UI that bypasses proctoring software.

## Target Platforms
The launch post is optimized for:
- **Reddit**: Specifically `r/cscareerquestions` and `r/jobs`
- **Product Hunt**
- **Twitter & LinkedIn**

## Key Features to Highlight
1. **100% Invisible**: Bypasses screen-recording software and Zoom/Teams sharing.
2. **Zero-Delay Answers**: Local processing eliminates awkward pauses.
3. **Resume Customization**: Injects user's background directly into the LLM context.
4. **Live Web Search**: Silently searches the internet for up-to-date knowledge during the interview.
5. **Remote Helper Mode**: Allows a friend to control the app remotely via a secure link.

## 💰 Optimized Pricing Strategy (Prepaid & BYOK)
- **Free Demo Trial**: 15-Minute Live Trial with Managed Keys (instant test drive).
- **30-Day BYOK Pass**: $10 (Prepaid for 30 consecutive days, Bring Your Own Key).
- **Annual BYOK Pass**: $50 (Prepaid for 365 days, Bring Your Own Key).
- **SaaS Managed Pass**: $19 (Prepaid for 8 full interview sessions, fully managed API keys).
