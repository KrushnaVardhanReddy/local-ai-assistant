You are tasked with implementing Phase 82: Audio & App Mode Decoupling.
Currently, the App Mode (Meeting vs Transcript) and the Audio Capture Mode (Speaker Only vs Dual Mode) are coupled into a single `appMode` state variable. We need to decouple these so users can run the AI Assistant (Meeting Mode) while simultaneously capturing their microphone (Dual Mode).

**THE GOAL:**
By splitting these into two separate toggles, we unlock a 2x2 matrix of possibilities for the user:
1. **Meeting + Speaker:** AI listens only to the other people and gives answers (Max stealth).
2. **Meeting + Dual:** AI listens to *everyone*, including the user. (This allows the AI to hear the user's questions for the Magic Wand feature).
3. **Transcript + Dual:** Raw, two-sided transcription with no AI interference.
4. **Transcript + Speaker:** Just transcribing what the other people say.
