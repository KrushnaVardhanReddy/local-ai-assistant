═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
- `uvicorn[standard]` installs `uvloop` and `httptools` for better async performance — always use the extras bracket.
- `python-dotenv` is listed here as a convenience for local dev even though config.py also does manual .env parsing — having both is harmless and lets developers use `dotenv` CLI tools.
- Do NOT pin `torch` — faster-whisper uses CTranslate2 which bundles its own optimized runtime and does NOT require a separate PyTorch install.
