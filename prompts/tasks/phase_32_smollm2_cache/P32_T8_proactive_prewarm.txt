PREREQUISITE: P32-T3

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
OBJECTIVE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Build a "Mind Reader" feature that proactively pre-warms the Semantic Q&A Cache 
before an interview starts. Given a candidate's resume and a job description, 
the app will generate the 50 most likely interview questions and their perfect 
answers, and inject them straight into ChromaDB. During the interview, these 
cached answers will be served instantly with zero latency.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
CHANGES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. In `backend/app.py`, add a new POST endpoint: `/api/cache/prewarm`.
2. The endpoint should accept `resume_text` and `job_description`.
3. Use the Cloud LLM (via `llm_client.py`) with a system prompt: "Generate a JSON array of the 50 most likely interview questions and concise perfect answers based on this Resume and Job Description."
4. Iterate over the JSON array, run each question through the `SmolLM2` encoder (from P32-T1) to get its vector, and bulk-insert the vectors + answers into the `qa_cache` ChromaDB collection (from P32-T3).
5. In `frontend/src/lib/Assistant.svelte`, add a "Pre-Warm Cache" button in the Settings panel (next to the Clear Cache button). When clicked, it should open a small modal to paste the Job Description (resume is already stored/known) and call the `/api/cache/prewarm` endpoint.
6. Show a loading spinner during the pre-warm generation, and a success toast ("Cache Warmed with 50 Questions") when done.
